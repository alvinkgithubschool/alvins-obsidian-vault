# Rieko

> **For future agent:** Collective space for onolonoo to design and create works, goods, and tools — collaborate with other entities. The most corporate-facing side of onolonoo: fast-moving, always changing. Formerly 'Design Works'. The studio practice and testbed of onolonoo: a commune and shared space for artistic and technical work. Pathfinders, builders, explorers.

## Focus Areas
- **Marketing** — Getting the work out there; selling products, collaborating with other brands, getting eyes on the design side
- **Talent** — Getting the best of the best on board to work together on different projects
- **Systems** — Organisation and tools for building, sharing, experimenting, and generating money

## Timeline — 1 Month (Jul 18 → Aug 18, 2026)
### Phase 1 — Conceptualisation (Jul 18 – Jul 25)
Lock in brand identity, service/product offering, talent model, systems stack. Define the marketing playbook and first outreach targets.

### Phase 2 — Page & Systems Build-out (Jul 26 – Aug 11)
Build the public-facing presence — website/portfolio page, internal systems for project intake and delivery, team onboarding process.

### Phase 3 — Launch (Aug 12 – Aug 18)
Go public. First post, first outreach, first project in motion.
