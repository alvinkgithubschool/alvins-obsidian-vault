# Strand

> **For future agent:** Framework for building experiences around music, interactive spaces, food and drink, and community. Building real experiences and creating space for others to share, borrow, and build together.

## Overview
A living thing — five months to find its shape. Strand builds self-sustaining experiential spaces where impermanence meets high-quality benchmarks. An avant-garde movement.

## Focus Areas
- **Spaces and Experiences** — Getting experiences up and running; a self-sustaining business model so spaces can thrive. Living in impermanence but with a high-quality benchmark.
- **Partners and Groups** — Connecting to others in this space to ease sourcing, exchange tools and work, borrow and learn together; pilot and coordinate the foundations of Strand across the world — from digital spaces to physical, and where they meet.

## Timeline — 5 Months (Jul 18 → Dec 18, 2026)
### Phase 1 — Conceptualisation (Jul 18 – Aug 1)
Define the experience framework, the business model skeleton, identify first locations and partners, map the digital-physical overlap.

### Phase 2 — Space Exploration, Diversification & Build (Aug 2 – Nov 18)
Talk to people, scout spaces, build partnerships, run pilot events, test the model in real conditions. Diversify formats. Standing up Strand's socials, digital presence, sub-pages, partner portals.

### Phase 3 — Launch & Consolidation (Nov 19 – Dec 18)
Formalise the model, lock in recurring partners, make the first real public moves.
