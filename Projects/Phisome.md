> **For future agent:** Phisome project - the canvas-space hub for onolonoo umbrella. A personal-first space that holds the synchrony layer between Are.na, Notion, and Obsidian.

## Overview
Phisome is the canvas-space hub that enables the synchrony layer between:
- **Are.na** (astrocrn/phisome-fzas0xfk1n8) - canonical text surface
- **Notion** (Cortex) - operational layer  
- **Obsidian** - local working surface + mirror

## Purpose
To provide a dependency-less, accessible, and intentional space for creative and critical work that respects the six Phisome pillars:
- Slow critical expansion (in kind not speed)
- Open access with limits (no bad use/exploitation)
- Create/design over hostile efficiency
- Dependency-less (core stands alone)
- Performance+accessibility (all hardware/abilities/backgrounds)
- Intentional engagement (consent, no content-delivery algorithms)
