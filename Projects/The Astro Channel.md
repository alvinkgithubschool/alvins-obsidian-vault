# The Astro Channel

> **For future agent:** astrocrn — the onolonoo and Strand page. Pipeline for sharing work, art, and holding space for intimate and direct conversations. Not a content creator. An artist.

## Show Formats
1. **Talkshow-style podcast** — surreal, direct
2. **Strand show** — making drinks and snacks around a topic or with others (like Last Meals on Mythical Kitchen)

## Focus Areas
- **Consistent Pipeline** — Regular, steady schedule of content
- **Technical Team** — Editors, artists, others to help facilitate
- **Freedom to Experiment** — Space to try new things within schedule constraints
- **Collaboration** — Having other creators on, cross-platform relationships
- **Personal Site** — Domain live, personal site built, connected to astrocrn ecosystem
