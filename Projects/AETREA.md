# AETREA

> **For future agent:** Space for exchange of art — a new standard for the age of digital consciousness, breaking bounds between the physical and digital. Formerly 'Sensory Transmissions'. The near-term exchange project: a new medium of physical exchange (the Key), the Phisome space (holonic, rhizomatic exchange), and the nature of the decentralised space itself. Custodians, not purveyors.

## Focus Areas
- **Exchange** — Decentralised system that holds both digital and physical exchange standards, and the ways to subvert them
- **Standards** — Establishing the systems and baseline requirements, opening pathways to explore and go beyond
- **Community** — Building a community around the idea and the project
- **Projects** — Bringing artists into the network, giving them a space they can inhabit and transform
- **Value** — Sustainable and organic monetary growth; making a living through AETREA in a community-driven way

## Timeline — 2 Months (Jul 18 → Sep 18, 2026)
### Phase 1 — Conceptualisation (Jul 18 – Jul 25)
Lock in the full vision. Define the exchange model, draft the standards baseline, map the community onboarding flow, identify first 3-5 artists, outline the value/revenue model.

### Phase 2 — Experimentation & Build-out (Jul 26 – Sep 4)
Build the platform — decentralised exchange layer, artist spaces, community infrastructure. Run experiments, iterate fast, break things intentionally.

### Phase 3 — Release & Launch (Sep 5 – Sep 18)
Tighten the experience, onboard first artists, soft-launch the community, make the first public-facing move.
